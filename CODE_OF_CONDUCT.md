# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex characteristics, gender identity, level of experience, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal or confidential information.
- Disclose security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for conduct they consider unacceptable. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers through a verified contact route.
